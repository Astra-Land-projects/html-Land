Astracraft

A self-contained voxel sandbox — no installs, one file. Break blocks, build, explore. Not a full Minecraft clone (no crafting tree, no multiplayer) — just the core sandbox loop, done properly.
WASD — move
Mouse — look around
Left Click — break block (hold)
Right Click — place block
1–9 / Scroll — select hotbar slot
Space — jump (Survival) / fly up (Creative)
Shift — sprint (Survival) / fly down (Creative)
F — toggle Survival / Creative
